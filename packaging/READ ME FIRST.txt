ARROWECH — says it again. and again.

INSTALL (easy way)
  1. Right-click "Install ArrowEch.command" and choose Open.
     (macOS asks because it came from the internet. Click Open again.)
  2. Rescan plug-ins in your DAW. Find "ArrowEch" by "Arrow".

INSTALL (manual way)
  Drag ArrowEch.vst3      into  ~/Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/VST3
  Drag ArrowEch.component into  ~/Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/Components
  Then in Terminal:
    xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine ~/Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/VST3/ArrowEch.vst3 ~/Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/Components/ArrowEch.component

STANDALONE
  ArrowEch.app runs without a DAW (drag it to Applications if you like).
  First launch: right-click > Open.

Works on Apple Silicon and Intel Macs, macOS 11+.
