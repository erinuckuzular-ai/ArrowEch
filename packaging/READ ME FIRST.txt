ARROWECH — says it again. and again.

INSTALL
  1. Double-click "Install ArrowEch.pkg".
     If macOS says it can't check it for malicious software:
     right-click the .pkg > Open > Open.
     (or System Settings > Privacy & Security > "Open Anyway")
  2. Follow the installer. Click Customize to pick VST3 / AU / Standalone.
  3. Rescan plug-ins in your DAW. Find "ArrowEch" by "Arrow".

INSTALLS TO
  VST3        /Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/VST3/ArrowEch.vst3
  AU          /Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/Components/ArrowEch.component
  Standalone  /Applications/ArrowEch.app

UNINSTALL
  Delete those three files.

Works on Apple Silicon and Intel Macs, macOS 11+.
